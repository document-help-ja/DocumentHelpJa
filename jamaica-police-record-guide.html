Don't Get Turned Away: The Essential Guide to Getting a Jamaican Police Record

Applying for a Police Record (Criminal Clearance Certificate) in Jamaica involves strict, unforgiving rules that differ significantly from other government applications. A single mismatch on your forms or photos will get you turned away at the station counter. Here is how to navigate the Criminal Records Office (CRO) smoothly so your file is accepted the first time.

By Laura Clarke

Lead Administrative Concierge

Key Takeaways

  • The name on your application must match your presented physical ID exactly, line for line.
  • Police record photos must be completely plain and uncertified. Do not use a Justice of the Peace (JP).
  • Certificates are legally locked to a specific recipient (for example, a specific embassy or employer) and cannot be reused for other purposes.
  • In-person fingerprinting is mandatory at a designated police station subsite.

The Step-by-Step Process

  1. 1

    Pay the TAJ fee

    You must pay for the record at a Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) office or through its online system before you can apply. Jump to Fees & Timelines

  2. 2

    Apply online

    Use the JCF's online portal to submit your details. You will be required to select the specific entity requesting the record, your target recipient.

  3. 3

    In-person fingerprinting

    Attend your scheduled appointment at your chosen subsite for fingerprinting and a brief interview. Bring all your prerequisites. Jump to Subsite Locations

  4. 4

    Collection

    Your printed certificate must be collected at your assigned pickup location. If you cannot return in person, authorised third-party document couriers or local proxy bearers can collect it on your behalf.

Major Rejection Traps

These two mistakes send more people out of the line than any others.

The Marriage Certificate Mismatch

If your official ID reads "Andrew Marcus Clarke" but you apply as "Andrew Clarke," you will be rejected. The same applies to marriage: if you apply under your new married surname but your physical ID still shows your maiden name, the clerk will turn you away. A marriage certificate does not bypass this rule. Your application must match the ID in your hand.

The Certified Photo Error

Passport and TRN photos must be certified by a JP. Police record photos must not be. Forensic technicians scan your image cleanly into the criminal database, so if a JP signs, stamps or embosses the back of your photo, it is ruined and you will be sent out of the line for fresh pictures.

C Overseas & Diaspora Applications

If you are outside Jamaica, you do not need to fly back to be fingerprinted. You can have your rolled fingerprints taken by a local police department in your host country, notarise your passport bio page, and courier the package to Jamaica for processing.

D The Apostille Add-on

If you are submitting this police record to a foreign government for immigration, visas or overseas employment, the raw document is usually not enough. You must have the record authenticated and stamped with an Apostille by the Jamaican Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFAFT) before it leaves the island.
