How it works

The step-by-step process

  1. Check for profile holds

    Make sure you have no outstanding court fines or unlinked legacy TRN profiles that could trigger an automated system block. See system blockers

  2. Complete your forms and pay

    Fill out Form F7. You can pay online at jamaicatax.gov.jm or in person at the cashier.

  3. Secure stamp duty (diaspora only)

    If renewing from overseas, your local representative must take your notarised authorisation letter to the Stamp Duty Office in Jamaica to be stamped (J$500 fee) before going to TAJ.

  4. Submission and driving compliance

    Submit your package at your chosen collectorate. You will receive an official TAJ payment receipt.

  5. Card collection

    Collect your printed card the same day or return after processing, depending on the office you chose. See the office directory

Keep your receipt in the vehicle. Your TAJ payment receipt together with your expired physical licence serves as your legal driver’s permit for up to 60 days while your new card is printed. Read the 60-day rule

Avoid these

Major rejection traps

These two mistakes cost people the most time at the counter.

The unstamped proxy letter

Submitting an overseas notarised authorisation letter directly to TAJ without first having it stamped at the Stamp Duty and Transfer Tax Office will result in immediate rejection at the cashier counter.

The historic ticket hold

Assuming a traffic citation from years ago was forgotten is a costly mistake. The automated TMS cross-reference blocks the transaction until official court clearance receipts are produced.

The 60-day legal driving rule

Many drivers worry about being penalised while waiting for their new card to print. Under Tax Administration Jamaica regulations:

  • Temporary compliance: the official TAJ payment receipt, kept together with your expired physical driver’s licence, is a valid legal permit to drive on Jamaican roads for up to 60 days from the date of payment.
  • Status tracking: if you applied at a drop-off location, you can track printing progress online with the Driver’s Licence Status Check tool on jamaicatax.gov.jm.
Official TAJ website jamaicatax.gov.jm Pay your renewal fee online and use the Driver’s Licence Status Check tool.

Collectorate directory: same-day vs drop-off

Where you submit your application decides how fast you get your card.

Same-day printing offices

You can receive your printed card the same day, often within a few hours, at these Revenue Service Centres (RSC) and tax offices:

  • Kingston & St. AndrewKing Street RSC, St. Andrew (Constant Spring) RSC
  • St. Catherine & St. AnnSpanish Town Tax Office, St. Ann’s Bay Tax Office
  • Clarendon & ManchesterMay Pen Tax Office, Mandeville RSC
  • St. James & WestMontego Bay RSC, Savanna-la-Mar Tax Office
  • St. Mary, Portland & St. ThomasAnnotto Bay Tax Office, Port Antonio Tax Office
  • PortmorePortmore Tax Office (renewal printing)

Non-printing drop-off offices

All other small tax offices accept renewal drop-offs. Applications are batched and sent to a regional printing hub, with a 20-business-day turnaround.

Reference desk

System blockers explained: TMS & legacy TRNs

Traffic Management System (TMS) holds

TAJ cross-references police and court databases in real time. If you have an unpaid ticket or a missed court date, the system locks your profile. Fines must be settled at the Traffic Court and cleared in the system before TAJ can issue a new licence.

Unlinked legacy TRNs

Older licences issued before central database integration often lack a direct digital link to your TRN. If the name spelling on your licence differs slightly from your TRN profile, the system will reject the transaction until a manual record pre-audit is done.

D
Reference desk

Medical assessment regulations (Form MC1)

To meet Island Traffic Authority (ITA) safety standards, some drivers must provide medical clearance:

  • Drivers over 70: must submit a completed Medical Certificate (Form MC1) signed by a registered physician confirming vision and physical fitness.
  • Commercial and general holders: drivers of public passenger vehicles (PPV), trucks or heavy equipment need a validated medical examination report.

The 90-day rule

Medical certificates are only valid for 90 days from the date of the doctor’s examination. A form older than three months when submitted to TAJ will be rejected.

E
Reference desk

Stamp duty & proxy rules for diaspora drivers

If you live abroad and cannot travel to Jamaica to renew, a local representative can act for you. However, TAJ will not accept a plain notarised letter.

  1. Stamp the letter

    Your notarised authorisation letter must be taken to the Stamp Duty and Transfer Tax Office in Jamaica and stamped. A J$500 government fee applies.

  2. Present the package

    Once stamped, your representative presents the validated letter, your notarised Form F7, your original expired licence and the TAJ renewal fee at the Revenue Service Centre.
