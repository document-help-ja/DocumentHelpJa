What is filing?

Telling TAJ once a year what your business earned and spent. It's all done online now.

Does filing mean I pay more?

Not always. Your business expenses come off your income first, so good records help you avoid paying tax you don't owe.

What happens when I message you?

You tell us what you need, we send an invoice, and we do it with you online. No office visit.

What filing does for you

  • Gets you a TCC, proof of income that can strengthen a visa application
  • Helps you qualify for NHT and bank loans
  • Makes opening a bank account easier
  • Builds your NIS pension for later
