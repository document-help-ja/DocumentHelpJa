Need an RGD Certificate?
Let's Find Your Lane.

Whether you need a certified copy of a birth, marriage or death certificate, or need an error fixed on an old record, we handle the RGD for you. Answer four quick questions below to find your lane.

Lane 1: 100% Concierge Retrieval

We can act as your proxy, apply for, and retrieve your certificates IF you meet all these criteria. Please verify below:

1. Are you requesting a standard certified copy of a Birth, Marriage, or Death certificate?

2. Is the event (birth, marriage, or death) already officially registered in Jamaica?

3. Do you have an old copy, OR know the exact name, date, and parish of the event?

4. Are you confident there are NO spelling errors or missing details on the original record?

⚠️ Your request needs specialised handling, so it isn't eligible for Concierge Retrieval.

Lane 2: Complex Cases & Record Amendments

If you answered "No" to any questions in Lane 1, your request requires specialized attention. We manage the process for you if you fall into these categories:

  • Correction of Errors: Fixing misspelled names, incorrect dates, or wrong gender markers on existing records.
  • Addition of Father's Name: Updating a birth certificate to officially include the father.
  • Late Registration: Registering a birth or death that was never officially documented.
  • Record Searches: Finding a certificate when the exact date or parish is unknown.
View Common Requirements by Scenario

Correction of Errors

  • Original certificate containing the error.
  • Supporting documents showing the correct information (e.g., Baptismal record, older child's birth certificate, or parent's marriage certificate).
  • A signed declaration form (we will provide this).

Addition of Father's Name

  • Original Birth Certificate of the child.
  • Valid Photo ID for both parents.
  • A Statutory Declaration signed by both parents in the presence of a JP/Notary.

Late Registration (Births)

  • Immunization Card or Baptismal Certificate.
  • Letter from the hospital/midwife where the birth occurred.
  • Declarations from older relatives who remember the birth.

Late Registration (Deaths)

  • Requirements depend on when and where the death occurred. We confirm the exact evidence list after reviewing your case.

Record Searches

  • The person's full name, and any other names they may have been registered under.
  • The parish, and an estimated date range for the event.
  • Parents' names (for births) or spouse's name (for marriages), if known.

RGD Certificate Questions

Which lane am I in?

Answer the four questions in Lane 1. If every answer is Yes, you qualify for Concierge Retrieval of a certified copy. If any answer is No, your request needs a record search, amendment or late registration, which we handle in Lane 2.

Can you get a certificate if I don't know the exact date of birth?

Yes. Without the exact date or entry number, your case falls into Lane 2. We request an official RGD record search using an estimated 10-year window.

I live overseas. How will I receive my certificate?

We collect the certificate from RGD and send it to you by courier. Courier costs are separate and depend on your country. Local clients can collect from us or have it delivered within Jamaica.

Can you get my certificate apostilled for use abroad?

Yes. Apostille is available as an add-on for certificates we retrieve. Message us on WhatsApp for the current fee and timeline.

How long does a correction of error take?

Amendments are legal processes and do not qualify for RGD's express service. Depending on the complexity of the error, they can take anywhere from 4 weeks to 6 months. We handle the follow-ups so your case stays active.

Can I request someone else's birth or death certificate?

Yes, Jamaican civil registration records are public. You can request a certified copy of another person's birth, marriage or death certificate, as long as you provide enough detail to locate the record.

Are government fees refundable?

No. RGD fees are paid to the agency and are non-refundable. Our service fee is separate and is covered in our Terms & Conditions.
