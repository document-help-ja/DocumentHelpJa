Your part
  • Give accurate names, dates and places, or upload an old copy, on our intake form.
  • Give your consent for us to act for you.
  • For adding a father's name: provide truthful, valid evidence and sign declarations before a JP or Notary Public.
  • Adding a father's name is a manual legal process. Timelines can't be guaranteed and may take several months.
  • RGD fees are non-refundable once paid. Our fee is covered in our Terms & Conditions.
Using the certificate abroad (apostille)

Many overseas authorities require an apostille. Add it to your retrieval so it's done in one trip. Ask about the apostille

How long does adding a father's name take?

It doesn't qualify for RGD's express service. Depending on the case, it takes from 4 weeks to 6 months. We follow up so your case stays active.
