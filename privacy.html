At Laura Clarke Administrative Services, protecting client privacy and personal identification data is paramount. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information in strict compliance with the Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA) and guidelines established by the Office of the Information Commissioner (OIC).

1. Information We Collect

To audit applications and perform administrative assistance on your behalf, we may collect:

  • Identity Information: Full legal name, maiden name, date of birth, TRN, and copies of government-issued identification (Passports, Driver's Licences).
  • Contact Information: Phone numbers (including WhatsApp messaging records), email addresses, and physical delivery addresses.
  • Documentary Records: Vital record certificate copies (Birth, Marriage, Death), motor vehicle registration data, fitness details, or official application forms.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your information is processed exclusively for executing the specific administrative service you have requested. We do not sell, trade, or share client data with external marketing bodies. Data is used to:

  • Pre-audit forms to prevent agency rejection due to name or data mismatches.
  • Submit applications on official government portals (PICA, TAJ, RGD, JCF).
  • Provide direct case status updates via WhatsApp, email, or telephone.
  • Coordinate secure doorstep courier delivery for finalized certificates.

3. Data Retention & Digital Purging

Because we process high-sensitivity personal identification documentation, we enforce strict retention boundaries:

  • Physical Papers: Original documents provided for local processing are either submitted directly to the relevant agency or returned to you via tracked courier. We do not retain physical original documents indefinitely.
  • Digital Records: Scans, photo uploads, and identity records submitted via email or encrypted WhatsApp are retained only for the duration of active processing. Once a case is completed and verified as delivered, all related digital scans are securely purged from active systems within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

Information is disclosed strictly to:

  • The specific Jamaican government agency handling your document request (PICA, TAJ, RGD, JCF).
  • Authorized courier operators (e.g., DHL, Zip Mail) solely for address dispatch fulfillment.

5. Security Safeguards

We employ physical, organizational, and electronic security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Digital communications involving sensitive document files are handled through secure channels.

6. Your Data Rights

Under the Jamaica Data Protection Act, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to errors, or request immediate purging of digital files upon service completion.
