Need a Jamaican Police Record?
Let's Find Your Lane.

Avoid counter rejections at the Criminal Records Office. Whether you are living in Jamaica or applying from overseas, answer four quick questions below to find your lane.

Lane 1: Local Applicants (In Jamaica)

We can fast-track your package pre-audit and Tax Office fee payment IF you meet all these criteria. Please verify below:

1. Are you currently physically located in Jamaica and able to attend fingerprinting?

2. Do you have a valid Jamaican Passport, Driver’s Licence, or National ID?

3. Do you have an active Jamaican Tax Registration Number (TRN)?

4. Can you provide two (2) uncertified passport photos meeting JCF strict plain-background rules?

Lane 2: Overseas Diaspora & Complex Cases

If you live abroad or are missing key prerequisites, you cannot apply directly at the counter. Laura Clarke Administrative Services acts as your local proxy in Kingston if you fall into these categories:

  • Overseas Applicants (Diaspora): Applying from the US, UK, Canada, or elsewhere using a physical fingerprint card.
  • Overseas Applicants Without a TRN: You can still apply. Without a TRN, the fee is paid through the JCF Finance Branch instead of TAJ, and we handle that for you.
View Requirements for Overseas Applicants

Overseas Applicant Checklist

  • Original physical Fingerprint Card taken and stamped by an authorized law enforcement agency (e.g., Police, FBI, or local state police).
  • Certified (notarised) colour copy of your valid passport bio-data page.
  • Two (2) recent, identical passport-sized photographs, not signed, sealed or certified.
  • Cover letter addressed to the Superintendent of Police, signed by you (we prepare it for your signature).
  • Your Jamaican Tax Registration Number (TRN), if you have one.
  • Signed Letter of Authorization empowering Laura Clarke Administrative Services to submit and collect on your behalf.

⚡ How We Help Overseas Applicants

Fingerprint cards mailed from overseas are frequently rejected due to smudges, formatting errors, or missing TAJ fee vouchers. We handle the entire local execution in Kingston.

Learn More About Our Process
  • Fingerprint Pre-Audit: We inspect high-res digital scans of your fingerprint card before you mail it to Jamaica to confirm clarity.
  • TAJ Voucher Payment: We pay your official government fee at Tax Administration Jamaica and attach official vouchers.
  • CRO Kingston Lodgment: We physically submit your audited file to the JCF Criminal Records Office in Kingston.

Police Record Questions

Which lane am I in?

Answer the four questions in Lane 1. If you are in Jamaica with valid photo ID, a TRN and compliant photos, you qualify for Lane 1 Local Fast-Track Preparation. If you are applying from overseas, you belong in Lane 2 Overseas Proxy Representation.

Can I get a Jamaican police record if I live overseas?

Yes. Overseas applicants must get their fingerprints taken on an official law enforcement card in their host country and mail the physical card to our Kingston office along with two photos and your authorisation. We pay the JCF fee, lodge your file at the CRO in Kingston, collect the certificate in person, have it apostilled and courier it to you.

Do I need to send my original passport?

No. Overseas applicants only need to send a certified notarized color copy of their valid passport bio-page, alongside their original physical fingerprint card.

Can you apostille my police record for use in Europe or the Middle East?

Yes. For overseas clients, the apostille is included in our fee: once JCF issues your certificate, we collect it in person and take it to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs & Foreign Trade (MFAFT) for its apostille before couriering it to you. Local clients can add it on request.

How much does a Jamaican police record cost, and how long does it take?

JCF charges $3,000 JMD for regular processing (21 working days), $6,000 JMD for express (5 working days) and $8,000 JMD for next-day service when JCF offers it. For overseas clients, add time for the apostille and courier transit after JCF issues the certificate. Our service fee is separate and shown in each lane above.

Are government fees refundable?

No. JCF and TAJ fees are non-refundable once processed. Our service fee is separate and covered in our Terms & Conditions.
