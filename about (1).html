About

Hi, I’m Laura. I’ve been stuck too.

I’ve stood in those lines, got stuck on those forms and hit the same roadblocks you have. When I applied for a Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) to open a business bank account, it was refused, because my own self-employed tax returns hadn’t been filed.

Why I do this

Today almost every Jamaican service runs through an online portal, but the steps aren’t always clear, and some aren’t written anywhere. I know where people get stuck. I find the missing step, explain it in plain words, and check the details carefully.

When people ask what I do, I say I’m a documentation consultant. I help people with their documents, on paper and online.

Care with your information is at the core of my work. I spent 14 years in banking, working under Know Your Customer rules, and I supervise national exams, where protecting confidential papers is the job. I bring that same care to your records. The details are in my Privacy Policy.

Questions people ask

Short answers. General information, not legal or tax advice.

Police records

Why do police record photos get turned away?

The police record needs one plain photo. Unlike a paper passport application, it must not be signed, stamped or embossed by a Justice of the Peace. A certified photo is a common reason people are sent away.

The name on your application must also match your photo ID exactly.

See the police record service

Can I use one police record for several applications?

No. A Jamaican police record is addressed to one recipient you name when you apply, such as a specific embassy or employer. It can’t be reused for anyone else, so confirm who it should be addressed to before you apply.

Passports

Do both passport photos need a JP’s signature?

No. It depends on how you apply:

  • Online renewal: you upload a digital photo. No JP signature or stamp is needed.
  • Paper application (first adult passport, children and name changes): you supply two photos, and only one is certified by a JP or Notary along with the form. The other stays plain.

Read the passport guide

Can someone submit my passport application for me?

For an adult renewal done online, yes: I can do it for you from start to finish.

For paper applications, including a renewal with a name change, you complete and submit the application yourself.

See passport renewal

Do I need to send you my original ID?

No. Once I’ve accepted your case, you send clear photos or scans of the documents I ask for. I don’t carry clients’ original IDs to any office.

For an overseas police record or driving abstract, you send a notarised letter of authorisation instead. For a police record from overseas, you also send a prepaid DHL envelope for the certificate’s return.

Vehicles and tax

Why is TAJ blocking my motor vehicle renewal?

The TAJ portal usually stops a renewal for one of two reasons:

  • Unpaid traffic tickets: outstanding tickets or court dates linked to your TRN.
  • Insurance not showing: your insurer hasn’t yet updated your cover in the central database.

My renewal service checks both before anything is paid.

See the motor vehicle renewal service

Why didn’t I get a windscreen sticker?

Since 21 July 2025, TAJ no longer issues a sticker. Your proof is the Electronic Motor Vehicle Registration Certificate (eMVRC), which you can view, save or print at any time on the TAJ Certificate Portal. Keep a copy on your phone.

Can you help with my S04 tax return?

My tax books take you through it step by step: getting your records ready, filing the S04 on the TAJ portal, and payroll if you have a worker. If your case needs a person, ask me about a consultation. For tax advice on your own figures, speak with a qualified accountant.
