      Things you might not know

      Bureaucratic rules change constantly. Here are a few recent administrative insights to save you time.

      Companies Office of Jamaica (COJ)
      • No More Physical Certificates: The COJ is no longer issuing physical registration certificates. When your new business is approved, the certificate is issued digitally and must be printed on your own.
      • Email Conflicts: When applying for a new business online, the email on your application must match the email on your personal COJ portal account, or the system will flag it as an identity conflict.
      Police Records (JCF) & Background Checks
      • Strict Name Consistency: The name on your online police record application must identically match the government ID you present at the interview (usually a passport). If your ID is in your maiden name and you apply in your married name, the application will be rejected.
      • Bridging Names: If your employer requires a record in your married name but your passport is in your maiden name, get HR clearance to apply in your maiden name and attach your official RGD marriage certificate.
      Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ)
      • Overseas TRN Applications: You do not need to fly to Jamaica to get a TRN. You can apply from the diaspora if your identity documents are properly notarized by a Notary Public in your current country and submitted by a representative in Jamaica.
