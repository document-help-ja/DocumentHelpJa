PICA

Jamaican Passport Services

First-time applications, adult renewals, minor applications and lost passport replacements.

Select your application type below to view its exact requirements, government fees, and concierge retainer.

Adult renewal (simplified, no name change)

What you need to provide:

  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate (or Proof of Jamaican Citizenship)
  • Current or expired original Jamaican passport
  • One (1) passport photograph (no JP signature required)
Government Fee $6,500 JMD
Concierge Retainer $5,000 JMD
PICA Turnaround 7 – 10 Business Days
Adult renewal (with name change)

What you need to provide:

  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate (or Proof of Jamaican Citizenship)
  • Current or expired original Jamaican passport
  • Official name change document (original marriage certificate or deed poll)
  • Two (2) passport photos (one certified by a JP or Notary)
Government Fee $6,500 JMD
Concierge Retainer $6,000 – $7,000 JMD
PICA Turnaround 7 – 10 Business Days
Minor (under 18) renewal

What you need to provide:

  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate of the minor
  • Valid government ID of the signing parent or guardian
  • Two (2) passport photos (one certified by a JP)
Government Fee $4,000 JMD
Concierge Retainer $5,000 JMD
PICA Turnaround 7 – 10 Business Days
Lost or stolen replacement

What you need to provide:

  • Original Jamaican Birth Certificate
  • Original police report detailing the loss
  • Two (2) passport photos certified by a JP
Government Fee $11,500 JMD
Concierge Retainer $7,000 – $8,000 JMD
PICA Turnaround 10 – 14 Business Days

JCF

Police Records & Clearance Certificates

Local fingerprint scheduling, overseas fingerprint submission and certificate pickup.

Select your filing method below to view requirements and government speed tiers.

Local application (hybrid assist)

What you need to provide:

  • Valid tax receipt and valid government ID
  • Two (2) passport-sized photographs

We pre-pay fees & schedule your fingerprint slot. You appear briefly for fingerprint capture, then we collect the finished certificate.

JCF Government Fee $3,000 (21-Day) / $6,000 (5-Day) / $8,000 (Next Day)
Concierge Retainer $5,000 – $6,000 JMD
JCF Turnaround Based on JCF Tier Selected
Overseas / diaspora application (DHL to concierge)

What you need to provide:

  • Rolled fingerprints taken by police agency in host country
  • Colour copy of valid passport bio page
  • Notarised authorisation letter
JCF Government Fee $3,000 (Regular) / $6,000 (Express)
Concierge Retainer $7,000 – $10,000 JMD
JCF Turnaround 10 – 15 Business Days
Using it overseas? Add an apostille +$3,000 – $5,000 JMD Add-On

Need this police record officially recognised abroad? Ask for apostille authentication during your intake call.

RGD

Birth, Marriage & Death Certificates

Official certificate searches, genealogical retrievals and registry corrections.

Select your service type below. Note: Copy applications require a known Entry Number; otherwise an RGD search is required.

Certificate copy / replacement (Entry Number available)

What you need to provide:

  • Official Entry Number (from old certificate or pink slip)
  • Copy of applicant's valid ID and TRN
  • Full names of subject and parents
RGD Government Fee $2,500 (Regular) / $6,000 (Express)
Concierge Retainer $4,000 – $6,000 JMD
RGD Turnaround 7 – 14 Business Days
Registry record search (Entry Number unknown / lost)

What you need to provide:

  • Full name of subject at birth/marriage/death
  • Approximate date and parish/district of event
  • Parents' full names (including mother's maiden name)
  • Copy of applicant's valid ID and TRN
RGD Search Fee $1,500 JMD
Concierge Retainer $4,000 – $6,000 JMD
RGD Turnaround 3 – 6 Weeks (Archive Search)

*Note: Once the Entry Number is located via search, standard certificate printing fees apply.*

TAJ / ITA

Driver's Licence Renewal & Replacement

Renewal of expired licences, lost card replacements and medical endorsement submissions.

Select your transaction type below to view exact TAJ fees and concierge retainers.

Class B renewal (private motor car)

What you need to provide:

  • Expired driver's licence card
  • Valid TRN and one (1) passport photo certified by a JP
  • *Medical Note: Applicants aged 70 or older (Form MC1) must include a doctor's examination report.
TAJ Government Fee $5,400 JMD (5-Year)
Concierge Retainer $4,000 – $6,000 JMD
TAJ Turnaround 3 – 5 Business Days

*Note: 60-day legal driving receipt issued immediately upon counter filing.*

Class C renewal (commercial / general)

What you need to provide:

  • Expired driver's licence card
  • Valid TRN and one (1) passport photo certified by a JP
  • Completed doctor's medical examination report (Form F7/MC1)
TAJ Government Fee $7,200 JMD (5-Year)
Concierge Retainer $4,000 – $6,000 JMD
TAJ Turnaround 3 – 5 Business Days
Substitute licence (lost or stolen replacement)

What you need to provide:

  • Valid passport (in place of missing card)
  • Valid TRN and one (1) passport photo certified by a JP
TAJ Government Fee $4,140 JMD
Concierge Retainer $4,000 – $6,000 JMD
TAJ Turnaround 5 – 7 Business Days

*Note: TAJ performs an internal ITA record verification before issuing substitute cards.*
