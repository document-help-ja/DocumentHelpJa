How We Work Together

A simple, transparent process from first message to finished document.

  1. 1

    Document Pre-Audit

    Send us a WhatsApp photo of your existing IDs and documents. We audit everything against exact agency rules before any filing fees are paid.

  2. 2

    Brief Consultation & Setup

    We complete application details over a brief call, set up portal entries and coordinate fee payments. Exact spellings and addresses are confirmed by text.

  3. 3

    Turnkey or Guided Filing

    Depending on agency rules, we either handle it 100% turnkey through to pickup, or use hybrid guided filing: a pre-audited file for any mandatory in-person biometric or fingerprint appointment.
