Before making another payment attempt

  1. Keep your application details.Save your reference, any receipt or confirmation, and the date and time you tried to pay.
  2. Save the exact message.A screenshot may help explain the problem. Keep the original private. Hide personal and payment details before sharing it outside an official secure support channel.
  3. Check with your bank and PICA.Your bank can explain a declined payment or a payment showing in your account. PICA can check your application and payment in its system.
  4. Confirm your next step.If access is blocked or a payment remains unclear, ask PICA how to continue. Do this before creating another application or paying again.

If your application is locked

Laura’s experience

After a failed payment, your application can stay locked for a while. In the cases we have seen, it was about two hours. Trying the same application again and again does not unlock it.

If you still cannot get in, contact PICA. Explain the error and ask how you can continue.

PICA handles application access. Laura’s service covers completing your online renewal and paying the fee.

Can you return to an application you already started?

PICA offers a Resume option for recently started applications.

Contact customer care if you started more than five business days ago and could not finish. Also contact them if you could not email the application for printing.

Keep your existing reference. Ask whether you can resume, need another way to complete, or must take another step.

If your bank shows a payment

A payment showing at your bank does not mean PICA has confirmed it.

Ask your bank what the payment entry means. Give PICA your application reference and relevant payment details through its official support channel.

What if more than one payment appears?

Report it promptly. PICA considers payment errors and refunds under its policy.

An error message does not guarantee a refund. It also does not guarantee when a payment will be reversed.

Keep your payment details private.

Never share your full card number, security code, PIN, banking password or one-time banking code in a public message.

Do not put these details in our enquiry form either.

If online payment cannot be completed

You can complete the process at a PICA office in Jamaica or a Jamaican overseas mission.

What should you prepare before going in person?

For this option, PICA lists:

  • Your printed application.
  • Your current passport.
  • Two professionally produced passport photographs.
  • The applicable fees.

Before you travel, contact the office or mission. Ask how they will handle your existing application.

Check whether you need an appointment or extra documents. Mention any payment your bank shows that PICA has not confirmed.
