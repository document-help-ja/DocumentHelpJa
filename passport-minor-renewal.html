Minor Passport Renewal: Form Prep & Vetting

PICA enforces strict child protection security rules. We complete your application forms as a print-ready PDF and audit your paperwork digitally so your in-person visit is guaranteed to go smoothly.

Strict PICA Legal Appearance Rules for Minors

To protect children against unauthorized passport issuance, PICA strictly enforces the following rules at all counters and Consulates:

  • The Child Must Attend: Any child who is over 3 years of age must physically attend the appointment in person. (Infants under 3 years old do not need to attend).
  • The Parent Must Attend: The adult accompanying the child must be a parent whose legal name appears on the child's birth certificate.
  • Father Attendance Exception: A father whose name is not on the child's birth certificate will be turned away by PICA—unless he presents a formal, notarized written authorization signed by the mother (whose name IS on the birth certificate).

What Documents We Need For The Audit

Before you travel to PICA or the Consulate with your child, we require clear digital copies of your document set. We will use this information to build your perfect application package.

  • Minor's Original Jamaican Birth Certificate (or official Proof of Jamaican Citizenship). The attending parent's name must be clearly listed.
  • Minor's Current or Expired Original Passport.
  • Parent's Current, Unexpired Government ID: Valid Driver's Licence, Passport, or National ID. The signature on this ID must perfectly match the signature you will place on the child's printed application form.
  • Two (2) Physical Passport Photographs of the Minor: One photo must be certified (stamped and signed) by a Justice of the Peace, Notary Public, or authorized official.
    View Photo & Certification Guidelines
    Photo Certification Rules:
    • The certifying official must write: "I certify that this is a true photograph of [Minor's Name]" followed by their signature and official seal.
    • Photos must have a plain white background, neutral facial expression, and no head coverings (unless religious).
  • Application Information: We will securely collect the necessary background details from you to professionally complete the official application form on your behalf.

How Our Form Prep & Vetting Service Works

Minor applications are frequently rejected at the counter due to typos, crossed-out mistakes on forms, or flawed certification stamps. We handle the paperwork to ensure your application is 100% error-free.

  1. Secure Digital Intake: We securely collect clear digital copies of the child's documents, the parent's ID, and the necessary application details.
  2. Professional PDF Form Preparation: We expertly fill out the editable PICA application form online on your behalf. This guarantees there are zero spelling errors, cross-outs, or invalid fields. We then send you the completed, ready-to-print PDF file.
  3. Printing & JP Signature: You print the completed PDF form. The legal parent signs the form in the presence of a Justice of the Peace (JP) or Notary, who will also certify the minor's photo and sign Section G of the form.
  4. Final Compliance Pre-Audit: You securely share a photo of the signed, certified packet with us. We conduct a final line-by-line audit of all seals and signatures to make sure PICA will accept it without issue.
  5. Ready for In-Person Submission: You take your certified, flawless application package and your child directly to the local PICA office, Jamaican Embassy, or Consulate for submission.
