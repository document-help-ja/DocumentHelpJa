About Laura Clarke Administrative Services

Your tax, licences and documents, handled online. No office visits, no lines.

Why I Built This Service

I've stood in those lines, got stuck on those forms and hit the same roadblocks you have. When I applied for a Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) to open a business bank account, it was refused, because my own self-employed tax returns hadn't been filed. That's how I learned to file online through the TAJ portal, and why I now help others do it right the first time.

Today everything I do is online. Most of these processes can now be done through government portals, and I know where people get stuck. I check your details carefully before anything is submitted, so you avoid the mistakes that cause delays and rejections.

Trust and security are at the core of my work. I spent 14 years in banking, working under Know Your Customer rules, and every year I administer exams for the Overseas Examinations Commission, where protecting confidential materials is the job. I bring that same care to your records. Your information is used only for the service you asked for, and your supporting documents are deleted automatically within 30 days of completion. The details are in my Privacy Policy.

Whether you're running your own business in Jamaica or handling your affairs from overseas, you send your details and I take it from there.
