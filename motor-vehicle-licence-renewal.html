Skip the lines at the Tax Office. Tell us what your vehicle needs below, and we will calculate your estimated TAJ fees, process the paperwork, and secure your new registration.

Calculate Fees & Find Your Renewal Service

Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) and the Island Traffic Authority (ITA) require strict prerequisites before a vehicle can be renewed. Answer the questions below to find your processing lane.

Vehicle Details & Eligibility

1. Is your Motor Vehicle Insurance currently ACTIVE and valid?

2. Is your Certificate of Fitness still valid (not expired)?

3. What is the engine size (cc rating) of your vehicle? (Check your logbook)

4. How long do you want to renew your registration for?

🛑 Active Insurance Required

Tax Administration Jamaica will not process a motor vehicle registration renewal or fitness examination without active insurance showing in their central database. Please renew your motor vehicle insurance policy first, then return to this page.

Renewal Questions & Special Circumstances

Is the emailed PDF registration certificate legally compliant?

Yes. Tax Administration Jamaica issues electronic Motor Vehicle Register copies (e-certificates). As long as you print it out and keep it in your vehicle, it is fully recognized by law enforcement.

Can I renew my motor vehicle registration for two years at once?

Yes. Tax Administration Jamaica allows owners of private motor cars to pay their registration upfront for a two-year period, effectively doubling the standard 1-year rate without a penalty surcharge.

Does the engine size (cc) affect my insurance or just TAJ fees?

Both. The cc rating printed on your logbook strictly determines your TAJ government registration category, and insurance companies also utilize your engine capacity to calculate your annual premium rates.
