Good to know

What are TAJ's registration fees by engine size?

Your TAJ government registration fee is tied to the engine capacity (cc rating) printed on your C88 Logbook. These are the 12-month base rates:

Private car, up to 1,199 ccJ$9,240
Private car, 1,200 to 2,999 ccJ$12,600
Private car, 3,000 to 3,999 ccJ$28,800
Private car, over 3,999 ccJ$43,650
Electric carJ$9,240
Motorcycle, up to 125 ccJ$3,690
Motorcycle, 126 to 500 ccJ$5,580
Motorcycle, over 500 ccJ$8,550

Motorcycle fees are TAJ's 12-month rates. Source: TAJ rates and fees, checked October 2026.

If your fitness has expired, TAJ's fitness fee is added. It depends on your vehicle class:

Private car or truck, and motorcycleJ$4,500
Commercial, and trailer and tractorJ$5,400

We calculate your exact fee before you pay.

Can unpaid traffic tickets block my renewal?

Yes. TAJ cross-references police and court databases. Unpaid traffic tickets or missed court dates tied to the registered owner's TRN or the plate number automatically block online registration until they are cleared.

Fines are settled at the Traffic Court, and the hold must be cleared in the system before the renewal can go through. Our motor vehicle guide covers this and the other rejection traps.

Not sure what is on your record? We can look up your traffic tickets from photos of the front and back of your driver's licence. The lookup is included in our service fee, or J$1,500 on its own.

Can I renew for 6, 12 or 24 months?

Yes. You choose how long to renew your motor vehicle registration:

  • 6 months: half the 12-month rate, for example J$6,300 for a standard 1.5L car.
  • 12 months: the standard renewal period.
  • 24 months: TAJ lets you pay two years upfront at double the 12-month fee, with no surcharge.

Why must I take the vehicle to the depot myself?

An expired fitness certificate requires a mechanical roadworthiness check. The Island Traffic Authority (ITA) must physically inspect the vehicle's lights, tyres and structure, and verify that the chassis (VIN) and engine numbers match the C88 logbook.

Our Fitness Pre-Pay and Registration service removes the Tax Office payment line and handles the final registration portal. TAJ needs your TRN and your vehicle class to take the fitness fee, so we ask for both. We get the fee paid, leaving you to drive through the depot inspection.
