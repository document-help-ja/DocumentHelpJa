Why must I take the vehicle to the depot myself?

Unlike a simple online registration renewal, an expired fitness certificate requires a mechanical roadworthiness check. The Island Traffic Authority (ITA) must physically inspect the vehicle's lights, tires, and structural integrity, and verify that the chassis (VIN) and engine numbers match the C88 logbook.

Our Fitness Pre-Pay & Registration service saves you hours of waiting by completely eliminating the TAJ Tax Office payment line and handling the final registration portal. We get the fees paid while you wait, leaving you to simply drive through the depot inspection.

Check Your Engine CC & Exact TAJ Fees

Your TAJ government registration fee is directly tied to the engine capacity (cc rating) printed on your C88 Logbook. Below are the official 12-month base rates:

Motorcycles$4,200 JMD
Private car (Under 1,199 cc)$9,900 JMD
Private car (1,200 - 1,999 cc)$12,600 JMD
Private car (2,000 - 2,999 cc)$18,900 JMD
Private car (3,000+ cc)$27,600 JMD

Note: If your fitness is expired, add the $4,500 JMD TAJ Fitness Fee to these totals.

6-Month vs. 12-Month Renewal Periods

You can choose how long to renew your motor vehicle registration:

  • 6 Months: Billed at exactly half the 12-month rate (e.g., $6,300 JMD for a standard 1.5L car).
  • 12 Months: The standard renewal period.
  • 24 Months: TAJ permits you to pay for two years upfront by doubling the 12-month fee. No surcharge applies, and it saves you from repeating the process next year.
