Good to know

How we process your TRN
  1. Digital intake. You send us your ID scan and details. We fill in your TRN application form and check your notary stamp.
  2. TAJ submission. We submit your signed, certified application in person at Tax Administration Jamaica.
  3. Collection. When TAJ issues your TRN letter, we collect the stamped original over the counter and email you a scan.
  4. DHL dispatch. We send the original letter to your overseas address by tracked courier.
Who this service is for

Jamaicans in the diaspora and non-nationals living overseas (US, UK, Canada, Cayman and elsewhere) who need a TRN for legal or financial business in Jamaica and cannot apply at a tax office in person.

Do I get a plastic TRN card or a letter?

TAJ no longer prints plastic TRN cards. It issues an official stamped TRN registration letter with your 9-digit number, which banks, attorneys and government agencies accept.

Does my ID copy need to be notarised?

Yes. TAJ requires overseas applicants to submit ID copies certified by a Notary Public or a Jamaican embassy or consulate where you live.

How long does it take?

Once we have your signed form and certified ID, TAJ processing typically takes 3 to 5 business days. DHL adds about 2 to 4 business days to reach you.

Do I need a TRN for citizenship or a passport?

No. PICA does not require a TRN for citizenship by descent or a passport. You will need one later for banking, land and motor vehicle business in Jamaica.
