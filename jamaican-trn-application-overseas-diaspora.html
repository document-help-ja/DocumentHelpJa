Jamaican TRN Application for Overseas Diaspora & Non-Nationals

Applying for a Taxpayer Registration Number (TRN) from abroad? We manage your Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) proxy application, document pre-audit, in-person lodgment, and worldwide DHL courier delivery.

Overview

A TRN is mandatory for buying land, opening bank accounts, inheriting assets, or conducting any financial transaction in Jamaica. Designed specifically for applicants unable to visit the Tax Office in person, we act as your official local proxy agent in Kingston.

Who Is This Service For?

This service is specifically tailored for Jamaican Diaspora members and non-nationals living overseas (US, UK, Canada, Cayman, etc.) who require a TRN letter for legal or financial business in Jamaica and cannot travel locally to apply.

Frequently asked questions

Do I get a plastic TRN card or a letter?

Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) no longer prints plastic TRN cards. They now issue an official, stamped TRN Registration Letter containing your 9-digit Taxpayer Registration Number. This official letter is legally accepted by all banks, attorneys, and government agencies in Jamaica.

Does my foreign ID copy need to be notarized?

Yes. TAJ strictly requires that overseas applicants submit ID copies certified by a registered Notary Public or a Jamaican Embassy/Consulate in your host country.

Do I need a TRN to apply for Jamaican Citizenship or a Passport?

No. The Passport, Immigration and Citizenship Agency (PICA) does not require a TRN for citizenship by descent or passport issuance. However, once you become a citizen or hold assets in Jamaica, you will need a TRN for banking, land, and motor vehicle transactions.

How long does the entire process take?

Once we receive your signed form and notarized ID, TAJ processing typically takes 3 to 5 business days. DHL international shipping adds another 2 to 4 business days to reach your door.
