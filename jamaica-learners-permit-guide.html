How to Get Your Jamaican Learner’s Permit: The Complete Step-by-Step Guide

The Road Code Test now comes first. Here is the exact order that gets you from Form H3A to a permit in hand, without a wasted trip to the depot or the tax office.

Overview

Your first step toward driving in Jamaica is a Provisional Driver’s Licence, better known as a learner’s permit. It used to be a simple payment at the tax office. Effective January 25, 2020, you must first visit an Examination Depot to do a Road Code Test before proceeding to any Tax Office.

Who qualifies for a learner’s permit

To apply, you must:

  • Be at least 17 years old.
  • Be able to read, since the ITA checks reading ability before the test.
  • Have no road traffic convictions in the last 12 months.

Book and pass the Road Code Test

You cannot walk in and sit the test. Book an appointment first, either by visiting your nearest ITA service hub or by emailing driverslicence@mtw.gov.jm.

If you book by email, the ITA needs your full name, gender, date of birth, TRN, telephone number, full address, and the service hub where you want to sit the test. Use the ready-made email below.

Study for the test: official sources

  • Road Traffic Regulations, 2022 PDF · Jamaica Parliament
    The current rules of the road, including traffic signs and signals.
  • The Road Traffic Act, 2018 PDF · Jamaica Constabulary Force
    The law the regulations come from. Useful for reference.
  • Island Traffic Authority Ministry website
    Official notices and updates on testing and licensing.

About the Road Code booklet: it is not published on any government website. Copies circulating online are often old editions from before the 2022 rule changes, so if you study from a booklet, make sure it is a current edition.

What to expect on test day

  • Format: The Road Code Test is a multiple choice test designed to evaluate your knowledge of the general rules of the road, road signs, road markings, and road safety.
  • Pass mark: A pass mark of 75% has been determined.
  • Cost: The cost for the test is included in the fee for the Provisional Driver's Licence, which means the test itself is free. Re-sits are offered without charge until further notice.
  • If you fail: You will be advised by the ITA when you should return to retake the test.

Beware of fake tests and permits

In September 2026 the ITA warned that people were being charged J$18,000 to J$25,000 for unofficial Road Code tests and bogus permits. Only the ITA administers the test, and the only fee in this process is the J$1,800 paid at TAJ. A permit obtained any other way is not valid.

Pay and collect at the tax office

Whenever the Tax Office is processing a Provisional Driver's Licence, a database shared by ITA and TAJ will be checked to verify if the applicant has already passed the Road Code Test. You will usually be handed a sealed envelope. Do not open it; a tampered envelope is rejected.

Once you pass, take the documentation to any Tax Office. The clerk confirms your pass in the system, you pay the current fee of J$1,800, and your Provisional Driver’s Licence is issued the same visit.

Rules for learner drivers

Your permit remains valid for up to one year after the date it was issued. While you hold it:

  1. Hold it for at least six months before you can apply for the full driver’s licence examination.
  2. Never drive alone. A driver who has held a full licence for at least three years must sit beside you in the front seat.
  3. Display “L” plates clearly on the front and back of the vehicle.

Frequently asked questions

What if my permit expires before I take my driving test?

You do not re-sit the Road Code Test. Renew the permit at the tax office. A new medical is generally only needed if you are applying for a Class C licence or adding weight to that class.

What if my permit is lost or damaged?

Apply for a replacement. The time you have already held your permit still counts, so the six-month wait does not restart before your driving examination.

Can I sit the test in one parish and pay in another?

Yes. You can take the Road Code Test at one ITA depot and pay for your permit at a tax office in any other parish.

I had a learner’s permit before the new procedure came into effect. Do I still need the test?

Yes. Any person seeking to obtain a Provisional Driver's Licence after January 24, 2020 will be required to pass the Road Code Test, even if the individual had been granted a Provisional Driver's Licence in the past.
