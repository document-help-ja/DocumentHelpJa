Frequently Asked Questions

Will unpaid traffic tickets block my vehicle registration renewal?

Yes. If your TRN has outstanding traffic tickets or open court dates, the TAJ portal will freeze processing. Send us your TRN via WhatsApp and we can audit your account status before submitting.

Which Tax Offices offer the fastest Driver's Licence card pickup?

Major hubs equipped with on-site digital signature and photo capture include Constant Spring, Cross Roads, Spanish Town, Portmore, and Montego Bay Revenue Service Centres.

Can someone else pick up my Driver's Licence card for me?

Because TAJ mandates in-person biometric verification and signature capture, the cardholder must appear in person for final card collection. Our concierge service handles all pre-audits and fee payments beforehand to ensure your visit is brief.
