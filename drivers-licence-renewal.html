Overview

Our Prep & Pre-Pay service allows you to become compliant on the road immediately. We handle the TAJ fee payment and form preparation digitally, saving you hours of waiting when you finally visit the Tax Office.

Renewal Eligibility Check

1. Do you have any unpaid Jamaican traffic tickets?

🛑 Licence Renewal Blocked by TAJ System

Under the Road Traffic Act, Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) will not accept payment or process a driver's licence renewal if you have outstanding traffic tickets or warrants attached to your TRN.

How the Ticket Audit Process Works:

  1. TRN Database Audit ($1,500 JMD): We perform a live lookup on the TAJ portal to retrieve your exact list of unpaid tickets, demerit points, and statutory due dates.
  2. Eligibility Check: We determine if your tickets can still be paid online or if they have passed the due date (which requires a court appearance).
  3. Custom Invoice: We send you an itemized breakdown of your exact TAJ ticket fines + our lookup retainer.
  4. Online Clearing: Once you approve, we pay and clear your fines online. Once cleared, you can return here, select "No, my record is clean," and proceed with your licence renewal!

How "Prep & Pay" Works

We eliminate the tedious paperwork and the long payment lines. You become road-legal the same day we process your file.

View the 4-step workflow
  1. Digital Intake: You WhatsApp or email us a photo of your expired licence and your details. We perform a quick TRN ticket check.
  2. We Pre-Pay TAJ: We prepare your application forms and pay the official renewal fees at the Tax Office on your behalf.
  3. You Are Road-Legal: We immediately email you the Official TAJ Receipt and your completed forms. Simply print the receipt and keep it with your expired licence—you are now 100% compliant to drive!
  4. Final Card Drop-Off & Pick-Up: At your convenience, you drop off your completed package (with your pre-taken photos) at the TAJ counter. Because your fees are pre-paid and your photos are attached, you skip the photography queue and cash lines entirely!
💡 Pro Tip for Tax Office Visits: Bring your own passport photos with your package! Bringing your pre-taken photos allows you to completely bypass the long photography queue and gives you the exact polished look you want on your ID. If you drop off your complete package at a Major Tax Office (e.g., Constant Spring), they can print your card for same-day pickup. Smaller regional offices will accept the drop-off package and issue your physical card a few weeks later.

Frequently asked questions

Will the police accept the receipt if I am pulled over?

Yes! Once the official TAJ fee is paid, you are compliant with the law. Keeping the printed receipt along with your physical expired driver's licence proves to any law enforcement officer that your renewal is legally processing.

Why should I bring my own passport photos?

Bringing your own passport photos lets you skip the Tax Office photography line entirely! It speeds up drop-off processing and ensures you get an ID picture you are happy with.

What happens if my driver's licence is lost, not expired?

If you cannot surrender the expired card, TAJ requires you to first visit a local police station to report it missing and obtain an official Police Report. You must bring that Police Report to the Tax Office along with our prepared application.

I am over 70. Do my photos need to be signed by the doctor?

No. While you are required to have the Medical Form (Form F7) stamped and signed by your doctor, your two passport photos simply need to accompany the application uncertified.
