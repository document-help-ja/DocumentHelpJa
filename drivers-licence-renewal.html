Why do renewals get blocked at the counter?

TAJ cross-references police and court databases in real time. If you have an unpaid ticket or a missed court date, the system locks your profile, and fines must be settled at the Traffic Court and cleared before TAJ can issue a new licence.

Older licences issued before central database integration often lack a direct digital link to your TRN. If the name spelling on your licence differs slightly from your TRN profile, the transaction is rejected until the record is checked. Our pre-audit catches both problems before you pay.

TAJ Licence Fees & Turnaround

TAJ government fees are paid directly to the agency. Renewal fees can be paid online; substitute (replacement) fees are paid in person at the tax office.

Class B renewal (private motor car, 5-year)$5,400 JMD
Class C renewal (commercial or general, 5-year)$7,200 JMD
Substitute licence (lost or stolen, paid in person)$4,140 JMD

Note: Same-day printing is available at designated Revenue Service Centres and tax offices. Drop-off offices batch applications to a regional printing hub, with up to a 20-business-day turnaround.

The 60-Day Legal Driving Rule

Your official TAJ payment receipt, kept together with your expired physical driver's licence, is a valid legal permit to drive on Jamaican roads for up to 60 days from the date of payment. This applies to renewals paid online too.

Keep the receipt in the vehicle, and collect your new card at TAJ within the 60 days.
