Lane 1: Direct Descent (Parent Route)

We can fast-track your PICA application ONLY IF you currently hold 100% of all required ancestral records in hand. Please verify below:

1. Were you born outside of Jamaica to at least one parent born in Jamaica?

2. Do you currently HAVE IN HAND your original long-form birth certificate showing your parents' names?

3. Do you currently HAVE IN HAND your parent's original Jamaican Birth Certificate?

4. Do all names and spellings match EXACTLY across both birth certificates?

Terms & Your Responsibilities

Because citizenship alters legal records, you will need to:

  • Mail your original, certified birth certificates to our Kingston office: yours and your Jamaican parent's (PICA does not accept photocopies). If you were born while your parents were married, include their original marriage certificate.
  • Provide a copy of your valid government-issued photo ID.
  • Provide two passport-sized photos taken within the last six months, each certified by a Notary Public or JP with the statement "I certify this to be a true photo of [your full name]", then stamped, signed and dated.
  • Understand that PICA citizenship verification is a manual government process. Regular processing takes 90 working days (45 working days expedited) and cannot be guaranteed.

Lane 2: Grandparent Route & Complex Lineage

If you failed the document alignment check above, or are claiming through a Jamaican grandparent, your file requires heavy reconciliation before PICA will accept it. We manage the process for you if you fall into these categories:

  • Grandparent Route: Your parent was born abroad to a Jamaican-born parent (your grandparent), so your parent was already a Jamaican citizen by descent when you were born. This needs an unbroken document chain across 3 generations.
  • Name Discrepancies: Your parent's name on your foreign birth certificate does not match their Jamaican birth certificate.
  • Missing Generational Records: A parent or grandparent's birth was never officially registered in Jamaica.

Citizenship Questions

Which lane am I in?

Answer the four questions in Lane 1. If you are applying through a parent, have all original documents, and the names match perfectly, you qualify for Lane 1. If you are claiming through a Jamaican grandparent, or your names don't match, you belong in Lane 2. If your parent's Jamaican birth certificate is missing, we retrieve it through RGD first.

Why is the citizenship service locked if I am missing a birth certificate?

PICA will immediately reject any citizenship application that does not include certified original Jamaican birth certificates for the ancestral line. We first locate and retrieve the missing records through our RGD service, then prepare your citizenship file.

Can I apply for a Jamaican passport at the same time as my citizenship?

Yes. Once PICA approves your citizenship and issues your certificate, we can seamlessly transition your file to our passport processing service so you receive both documents.

What if my parent was born in Jamaica but passed away before I applied?

You can still claim citizenship by descent. You will need to provide their certified Death Certificate to complete the chain of custody alongside their Jamaican birth certificate.

What if my father's name isn't listed on my foreign birth certificate?

If you are claiming descent through your father, his name must be on your long-form birth certificate. If it is missing, you must formally amend your foreign birth certificate in your country of birth before applying in Jamaica.

Do I need to send original foreign birth certificates to Jamaica?

Yes. PICA requires original, certified long-form birth certificates from your country of birth. They will review them and we will return the originals to you along with your new Citizenship Certificate.

How much does Jamaican citizenship by descent cost, and how long does it take?

PICA charges $10,000 JMD for regular processing (90 working days) or $15,000 JMD for expedited processing (45 working days). Our service fee is separate and shown in each lane above.

Are government fees refundable?

No. PICA processing fees are non-refundable once the application is submitted. Our service fee is separate and covered in our Terms & Conditions.
