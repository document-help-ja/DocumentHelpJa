Why must I mail my original documents to Jamaica?

PICA maintains strict zero-tolerance policies for documentary fraud. They absolutely do not accept photocopies, scans, or digital prints for Citizenship by Descent applications. You must submit the original, physical, long-form birth certificates and marriage certificates.

When you use our Proxy Lodgment service, you courier your precious family documents directly to our secure Kingston office. We hand-deliver them to the PICA agent, track the application throughout the multi-month processing period, and then securely courier all your originals back to you along with your new Citizenship Certificate.

Missing Jamaican Birth Certificates (RGD Fetching)

If you are claiming citizenship through a parent or grandparent but do not have their original Jamaican birth certificate, your PICA application will be instantly rejected.

We offer a dedicated RGD Search & Retrieval Add-on ($50 USD per record). We physically go to the Registrar General's Department to search the national archives, print the modern computerized certificate, and attach it directly to your citizenship file.
