Citizenship by descent

How do I claim Jamaican citizenship through a parent or grandparent?

You apply to PICA in Kingston, or through the Jamaican mission that covers where you live. You show your family line with original birth certificates, add two certified photographs and your photo ID, and pay PICA's fee. Every name has to match, from the Jamaican birth certificate down to yours.

This is a paid consultation with a written guide and checklist. You gather your documents and submit the application yourself. You do not get a citizenship certificate from us.

  • Submitting the application at PICA or a mission

Reference & Resources

Who qualifies for Jamaican citizenship by descent?

You qualify if you were born outside Jamaica after 5 August 1962 and your mother or father was a Jamaican citizen by birth, descent or registration on the day you were born. If you were born before 6 August 1962, you qualify if your mother or father became a Jamaican citizen on that date, or would have but for their death.

A claim through a grandparent is proved link by link, with a birth certificate for each person in the line.

PICA's Fees & Processing Times

PICA charges a processing fee for citizenship by descent. You pay it yourself when you submit, and it is not refunded.

Missing a Jamaican Birth Certificate?

PICA asks for the original birth certificate of your Jamaican parent, and of your grandparent if your claim runs through them. If one is missing, we can retrieve it from the Registrar General's Department as a separate service: RGD certificates.
