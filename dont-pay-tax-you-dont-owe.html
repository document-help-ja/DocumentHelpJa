You have your figures. Now the portal is in front of you, and you’re not sure where anything goes.

The S04 is now filed on the TAJ portal. Many people log in, see tab after tab of boxes, and stop. Others have filed before but still wonder whether that one figure went in the right place. The help videos run for hours, and there’s nothing written to follow, page by page, with the screen in front of you.

This is that page-by-page guide. It takes you from your prepared records to a submitted S04 and S04A, one screen at a time, following Marcia’s figures all the way through.

What’s inside

  • Logging in, setting up eServices and giving a preparer access
  • Opening the right return and finding your drafts
  • Every tab of the S04, line by line, with clear drawings of each screen
  • Your job and your P24, without counting your pay twice
  • The S04A and your quarterly payments
  • “If you see this, it means that” checks, and a checklist before you click Submit

Who it’s for

  • First-time filers
  • People who’ve filed before and want to be sure this time
  • Preparers who file for others

Written for self-employed people.

What it won’t do

  • It doesn’t cover paying your tax or getting a Tax Compliance Certificate.
  • It isn’t tax advice.
  • Portal screens change from time to time, so always go by what’s on your own screen.
