PART 1: TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Scope of Services & Document Dispatch

Laura Clarke Administrative Services ("we," "us," or "the Service Provider") provides remote administrative, operational, document preparation, and business support services to clients ("Client" or "you"). The specific scope of work, deliverables, and requirements will be outlined in individual service agreements or written communication prior to commencement.

Document Dispatch: For security and compliance, all completed documents are dispatched directly from the issuing government agency to the verified applicant. We do not hold, collect, apostille, or forward these documents on your behalf.

2. Fees & Payment Terms

  • Payment Methods: Payment is accepted strictly via direct bank transfer to our designated local NCB account. We do not accept cheques, cash deposits, PayPal, Stripe, or overpayments.
  • Cleared Funds Required: Processing and the payment of government fees will only begin after your funds have fully cleared in our account. A screenshot of a transfer receipt does not qualify as proof of cleared funds. We never pay for a client out of our own accounts.
  • Who May Pay: Payment must come from the verified client named on the service request, from a bank account in that client's own name. We do not accept payments from third parties unless explicitly agreed upon in writing before payment is made.

3. Client Responsibilities

To ensure seamless delivery of services, the Client agrees to:

  • Provide timely access to all accurate files, credentials, forms, and personal information required to complete the agreed tasks.
  • Maintain clear communication and provide timely feedback or additional documentation if requested.

4. Confidentiality

We treat all client data, personal documents, business materials, and communication with strict confidentiality. We will not share, sell, or disclose your confidential information to any third party without your explicit consent, except as strictly required to process your applications with relevant agencies or as required by law.

5. Intellectual Property

Upon full payment, any custom work products created specifically for the Client become the property of the Client. Laura Clarke Administrative Services retains ownership of pre-existing templates, guides, checklists, and operational processes used to generate deliverables.

6. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Before Work Begins: If you cancel before we start processing, we refund the full amount you paid.
  • After Work Begins: Our service fees are non-refundable once work has started. Government and agency fees already paid on your behalf cannot be refunded by us, as these are paid directly to the agency.
  • Cancelling Your Request: You may cancel by giving written notice by email to info@documenthelpja.com.
  • If We Cancel or Stop Your Service: We may decline or stop any service at our discretion, including where information cannot be verified or a request falls outside our services. If we do, we refund any amount not yet spent on your behalf.
  • Fraud Prevention & Overpayments: To comply with anti-fraud security measures, we do not manually refund overpayments. If we receive more than the invoiced amount, suspicious transfers, or a payment from an unverified third party, all service processing will be immediately suspended. Excess or unauthorized funds will be reported directly to the bank for official reversal.
  • How Refunds Are Made: Any approved refunds are processed strictly back to the exact originating bank account. We will never issue refunds to third parties, alternative accounts, or via cash. Refunds are made within 10 business days, minus any unrecoverable bank charges.
  • Legal Obligations: We comply with the Proceeds of Crime Act (POCA). We may hold, decline or report any payment we believe to be suspicious, as required by law.

7. Limitation of Liability

Laura Clarke Administrative Services works with due care and diligence to ensure your submissions are accurate and compliant. However, we are not liable for indirect or consequential damages resulting from third-party or government processing delays, application rejections due to undisclosed client history, or platform disruptions. Our total financial liability is limited to the administrative service fee paid directly to us.

PART 2: PRIVACY POLICY & DATA RETENTION

Laura Clarke Administrative Services is committed to protecting your personal data in strict compliance with the Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA) and guidelines established by the Office of the Information Commissioner (OIC).

1. Information We Collect

To audit applications and perform administrative assistance on your behalf, we may collect:

  • Identity Information: Full legal name, maiden name, date of birth, TRN, and copies of government-issued identification (Passports, Driver's Licences).
  • Contact Information: Phone numbers (including WhatsApp messaging records), email addresses, and physical delivery addresses.
  • Documentary Records: Vital record certificate copies (Birth, Marriage, Death), motor vehicle registration data, fitness details, or official application forms.
  • Billing Information: Payment receipts, bank transfer confirmations, and transaction records.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your information is processed exclusively for executing the specific administrative service you have requested. We do not sell, trade, or share client data with external marketing bodies. Data is used to:

  • Pre-audit forms to prevent agency rejection due to name or data mismatches.
  • Submit applications on official government portals (e.g., PICA, TAJ, RGD, JCF).
  • Provide direct case status updates via WhatsApp, email, or telephone.
  • Coordinate secure doorstep courier delivery for finalized certificates.
  • Process invoices and maintain records for legal and tax compliance.

3. Data Retention & WhatsApp Intake Policy

Because we process high-sensitivity personal identification documentation via digital channels like WhatsApp and email, we enforce strict retention boundaries:

  • Digital Records & Scans: All client-submitted materials are retained strictly for the duration necessary to complete the requested administrative service. All physical and digital copies of sensitive supporting documents are permanently deleted and purged from our systems within 30 days of service fulfillment.
  • Physical Papers: Original documents provided for local processing are either submitted directly to the relevant agency or returned to you via tracked courier. We do not retain physical original documents indefinitely.
  • Security & Auditing: We employ organizational and electronic security measures to protect your data. Record-keeping logs of data destruction (metadata only) are maintained for internal compliance audits.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We only share data with trusted third parties as strictly necessary to fulfill our business contract with you. Information is disclosed strictly to:

  • The specific Jamaican government agency handling your document request (PICA, TAJ, RGD, JCF).
  • Authorized courier operators (e.g., DHL, Zip Mail) solely for address dispatch fulfillment.
  • Banking institutions necessary for processing official payments.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the Jamaica Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Request the immediate purging or deletion of your digital files upon service completion (subject to legal or tax retention obligations).
  • Withdraw your consent for data processing at any time.
