Adult renewal

How do I renew my Jamaican passport?

Most adults can renew through PICA's online portal without visiting an office: you need your current passport, a Jamaican citizenship document, a digital photo and a Visa or Mastercard. The breakdown: what PICA requires

What PICA requires for a digital application

  • You were an adult when your current passport was issued, and it was issued after September 2001.
  • The passport has expired or expires in less than one year, and you still have it.
  • Your name has not changed, and the passport is not lost, stolen or damaged.
  • Your expired or expiring passport.
  • A Jamaican citizenship document: birth certificate, adoption certificate or citizenship certificate.
  • A digital passport photo. Photos for a digital application do not need to be certified.
  • A Visa or Mastercard debit or credit card, and a working email address.

Source: PICA, Applying for a Passport and the online renewal portal.

What we do, and what you send us

  • You send clear images of your passport and citizenship document, plus your photo.
  • We check the photo against PICA's rules and its online photo checker before applying, so a bad photo is caught before it delays you.
  • We complete and submit the application, then send you confirmation and PICA's receipt.
  • We track the application and update you. On regular service the passport is usually printed by about the fourth business day.
  • PICA couriers the new passport to your address, or you collect it at a PICA office.

If a digital application isn't open to you

Adults whose last passport was issued when they were under 18, holders of older handwritten passports issued before September 2001, and anyone with more than a year left on the passport must apply on paper at a PICA office or a Jamaican embassy, high commission or consulate. With no name change you use PICA's simplified adult renewal form, which needs no certification, unless your passport pages are used up.

That is a preparation package: we audit your documents and prepare it, and you submit it. Message us for a quote.

Under 18, new or renewal

How do I get or renew a Jamaican passport for a child?

A parent or legal guardian applies on paper for anyone under 18, whether it is a first passport or a renewal; children's passports cannot be done online. Every child aged three or older must attend when the application is submitted, with the parent or guardian who completed the form.

Lost or stolen

How do I replace a lost or stolen Jamaican passport?

Report the loss immediately to the nearest police station or to PICA. Before you can apply for a replacement you also make a report to an officer in PICA's Investigation and Surveillance Unit, and PICA investigates. You then apply on paper with the report and a full set of identity documents. A passport reported lost or stolen is no longer valid for travel, even if you find it later.

Also applies to your application

What are the Jamaican passport photo requirements?

PICA asks for two identical colour photos with a matte finish, taken within the last six months against a plain background, showing a full frontal view of the head, neck and top of the shoulders.

Full photo rules

  • Size: the face measures 25mm to 35mm, with a 3mm to 4mm margin between the head and the edge of the photo.
  • Background: light complexions avoid white clothing and use a pastel background; dark complexions wear coloured clothing against a white background; grey or white hair uses a pale blue or pastel background.
  • Clothing: no exposed shoulders or chest. Bright colours are not recommended.
  • Glasses: no tinted lenses, and avoid heavy frames.
  • Headgear: religious reasons only, with Section H of the form completed. The face must show clearly from chin to forehead.
  • Quality: no shadows, glare, creases, ink marks, or other people or objects.
  • No embossed seal on the photo.
  • Digital applications use one photo file, which is not certified. Test it in the photo checker on PICA's portal before applying.

Source: PICA, Passport Photo Requirements.

Who can certify a Jamaican passport application?

In Jamaica, the certifier must be a Jamaican citizen who is not a family member and has known you personally for at least 12 months, such as a Justice of the Peace, attorney-at-law, medical practitioner or school principal. Certifying is free in Jamaica.

Full list and what they must write

In Jamaica: Member of Parliament, Justice of the Peace, attorney-at-law, medical practitioner, dental surgeon, veterinarian, principal of a primary, secondary or tertiary institution, police officer of gazetted rank, army officer of the rank of major or above, public officer at SEG 1 and above, Clerk of Courts, High Court judge, Resident Magistrate, bank manager, credit union manager, parish councillor, consular officer, marriage officer, Commissioner of Oaths or Notary Public, and passport officer.

Overseas, the certifier does not need to be Jamaican: Justice of the Peace, attorney-at-law, Notary Public, medical doctor, or minister of religion authorised to perform marriages. Your nearest Jamaican mission can advise on others.

On the photo: the same official signs the back of one photo under the words "I certify that this is a true photograph of [applicant's name]", with the date. A JP uses a seal at Section G of the form, never on the photo.

Not needed for a simple adult renewal. If your name has not changed, PICA no longer requires the form or photo to be certified.

Source: PICA, Who can certify the passport application.

How long does a Jamaican passport take?

PICA's regular service is 7 working days for applications submitted at its Kingston head office, 11 at Portmore and 14 at regional offices. The digital applications we make usually arrive in 7 to 14 business days, courier included.

Expedited and overseas times

  • Kingston head office only: same-day, next-day and three-day service, at higher fees.
  • Regional offices and outposts: five-day and seven-day expedited service.
  • Online, applying from Jamaica: a three-working-day expedited option.
  • Overseas applicants: no expedited service. Paper applications through a Jamaican mission take about 20 working days after they reach Kingston.

These are PICA's processing targets, counted from the day PICA accepts the application. PICA can ask for more documents, which pauses the clock. We cannot promise a delivery date, so start well ahead of any travel.

Source: PICA, Passport Fees and Applying for a Passport.

More passport questions

Can I renew my Jamaican passport before it expires?

Yes. PICA accepts digital applications once the passport has expired or has less than one year left. With more than a year left a digital application is not open to you, and you would apply on paper at a PICA office or Jamaican mission.

Can I renew my Jamaican passport from overseas?

Yes. Adults who meet PICA's online conditions renew through the online portal from anywhere and have the passport couriered to them. Everyone else applies on paper through the nearest Jamaican embassy, high commission or consulate. PICA offers no expedited service to overseas applicants.

Do I need a Justice of the Peace to sign my renewal?

Not for a straightforward adult renewal. If your name has not changed, PICA no longer requires the form or photo to be certified. Name changes, children, first passports and replacements all need a certifying official.

How will I receive my new passport?

For digital applications, PICA couriers the passport to your address or you collect it at a PICA office. For name-change renewals that we submit for you, the new passport is delivered to you by courier. For paper applications you submit yourself, you collect or receive it as the office or mission where you submitted directs.

Can you guarantee my passport will arrive by a certain date?

No. We make or prepare your application promptly, but PICA handles processing and the courier handles delivery. Start well ahead of any travel date.

Are PICA's fees refundable?

No. PICA's fees are paid to the agency and are non-refundable. Our service fee is separate and is covered in our Terms & Conditions.
