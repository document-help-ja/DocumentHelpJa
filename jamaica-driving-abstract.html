Overview

A Driving Abstract details your licence issue dates, traffic violations, active points, and suspensions. It is essential for lowering your car insurance premiums abroad or applying for commercial driving jobs.

Workflow

How We Process It

Because government offices will not mail abstracts directly to overseas clients, we perform the entire physical footwork on your behalf.

View the 5-step proxy workflow
  1. Document Pre-Audit: We vet your authorization letter and licence scans to ensure there are no errors.
  2. Stamp Office Presentation: We physically take your authorization letter to the Stamp Office to pay the legal stamp duty and receive the official stamp required for TAJ proxy representation.
  3. TAJ Lodgment & Payment: We present the stamped authorization at Tax Administration Jamaica, pay the official abstract fee, and lodge the request.
  4. Physical Collection: When the Traffic Authority prepares the official abstract, we return to the office to physically pick up the stamped document.
  5. DHL Dispatch: We personally take your document to DHL and send it via international tracked courier straight to your door.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my authorization letter need to go to the Stamp Office?

Under Jamaican tax laws, Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) will not allow a third party to request or collect a confidential driving record on your behalf unless the authorization letter bears an official stamp from the Stamp Office proving legal duty was paid.

Will the Tax Office send the abstract to me directly via DHL?

No. Government offices in Jamaica do not offer international courier dispatch services. We must physically return to the Tax Office to pick up your printed abstract over the counter and personally take it to DHL for dispatch.

Can you just email the abstract to me?

We will email you a scanned copy for your records the moment we collect it. However, foreign DMVs and insurance companies almost always require the physical, officially stamped original document, which we will mail to you via DHL.

I only had a Learner's Permit. Can I get an abstract?

No. A driving abstract is only generated for individuals who hold, or have previously held, a full Jamaican Driver's Licence.
