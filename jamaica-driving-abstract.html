Good to know

How we process your request
  1. Pre-audit. We check your letter and licence scans for errors before anything is paid.
  2. Stamp Office. We take your authorisation letter to be stamped, so it is accepted for representation.
  3. Payment and lodging. We pay the abstract fee at TAJ and lodge your request.
  4. Collection. When the Island Traffic Authority has prepared your abstract, we collect it in person and email you a scan.
  5. DHL dispatch. We send the original to you by tracked international courier.
Why does my letter need to be stamped?

Your driving record is confidential. A third party can only request and collect it for you with an authorisation letter that has been properly stamped. A plain letter is turned away at the counter.

Will the government send it to me overseas?

No. Government offices in Jamaica do not courier abstracts overseas. The abstract is collected over the counter, so someone in Jamaica has to pick it up and send it to you.

Can you just email it to me?

We email you a scan as soon as we collect it. Foreign licensing offices and insurers usually want the physical original, which we send by DHL.

I only had a learner's permit. Can I get an abstract?

No. A driving abstract is only issued to people who hold, or have held, a full Jamaican driver's licence.

Reference & Resources

In Jamaica and want to do it yourself? Here's what you need to know.

What a driving abstract shows

An abstract is your official driving history. It lists your licence issue dates and class, traffic violations, demerit points and any suspensions.

It is commonly requested by overseas licensing offices when you exchange your licence, by insurers who give discounts for driving experience, and by employers hiring drivers.

Getting your abstract yourself
  1. Pay the fee at TAJ. Pay the driving abstract fee at any tax office and keep your receipt.
  2. Go to the Island Traffic Authority. Take the TAJ receipt and your valid driver's licence to the ITA head office on Maxfield Avenue, Kingston.
  3. Collect your abstract. The ITA prepares the abstract and tells you when to collect it.

The abstract fee reported by the ITA is J$3,000. Fees can change, so confirm the current amount at the tax office before you pay.

Sending someone to collect it for you

Someone else can handle the request for you, but only with an authorisation letter from you. A plain signed letter is not enough: the ITA has said the letter must be signed and stamped by a Justice of the Peace.

Check the current requirements with the ITA before your representative goes, as a letter in the wrong form is turned away.

Unpaid tickets and your record

Unpaid tickets and demerit points appear on your abstract. If you need a clean record, settle outstanding tickets before you request it.

Older tickets may need to be cleared at the Traffic Court. Our driver's licence guide explains how ticket holds work.
