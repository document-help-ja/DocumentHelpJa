No Passport or Driver's Licence? How to Get a Jamaican TRN Using Only a Birth Certificate

Many individuals believe that lacking a Passport, Driver’s Licence, or National Voter's ID completely blocks them from obtaining a Taxpayer Registration Number (TRN). This is a very common misconception that leaves people feeling stuck.

Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) actually provides a clear, official alternative verification path for citizens without standard photo IDs. You just need to know exactly how to prepare the paperwork so the counter clerk accepts it on the first try.

What exactly do I need if I don't have a standard ID?

To use the alternative path, you must pair your original RGD Birth Certificate with a specially certified photograph and an official declaration form. First, obtain one standard passport-sized photograph of yourself.

Take this photo to a Justice of the Peace (JP), Doctor, Attorney, or Minister of Religion. They must endorse the back of the photograph by writing: "Certified true photograph of [Your Name]", followed by their signature and the date. A plain signature on the back is not enough; the exact wording matters.

Where do I find the Declaration Form, and what does it look like?

Next, you will need the TAJ's official "Declaration by Certifying Official" form. You can pick a physical copy up from the inquiry desk at any Tax Office, or you can download it directly below.

It is a straightforward, single-page document designed to formally link your face to your legal name. The official who certified your photograph must also complete and stamp this form. They will need to fill in your full legal name, state exactly how many years they have personally known you, sign it, and press their official seal or stamp onto the document.

Crucial Rule: The residential address written on the declaration form must match the address entered on your main TRN application line-for-line. If there is a discrepancy, your application will be rejected.

Can I have someone drop this off for me?

Because you are relying on this alternative photo verification method rather than a standard government ID, you cannot send a bearer or family member to drop off your application.

You must appear in person at the Tax Office counter. This allows the TAJ clerk to physically look at your face and verify it directly against the certified photo and JP declaration you provided.
