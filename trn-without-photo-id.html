How to Get a TRN in Jamaica Without Photo ID: The Birth Certificate Route (Adults, Children & Overseas)

Many people believe that without a passport, driver’s licence or voter’s ID, they can’t get a Taxpayer Registration Number (TRN). Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) actually provides an official alternative path using your birth certificate. Here is exactly how to prepare the paperwork, including the photo wording TAJ requires, so the counter clerk accepts it on the first try.

The step most people get wrong

Certify your photo with TAJ’s exact wording

Take one professionally produced passport-size photo to an approved official. On the back, they must write TAJ’s wording, then add their name, title, signature, the date and their official stamp.

Photo endorsement wording

Certified to be a true photograph of [Your Full Legal Name]

Show this to the official so they write it word for word, then add their name, title, signature, date and stamp.

Who can certify your photo

  • Justice of the Peace (JP)
  • Minister of Religion / Marriage Officer
  • Judge / Resident Magistrate
  • Attorney-at-Law
  • Member of Parliament
  • Parish Councillor
  • Medical Doctor
  • School Principal
  • Civil Servant (SEG3 and above)
  • Police Officer (Inspector or above)

Timing matters: the certification must be dated within the last six months to be accepted.

Links your face to your name

The Declaration by Certifying Official

TAJ requires every certified photo to come with a declaration from the certifying official. It is a single page that formally links your face to your legal name. The same official who certified your photo completes it: your full legal name, how many years they have personally known you, their signature, and their official seal or stamp.

Pick up a blank copy at the inquiry desk of any tax office, print it from the TAJ website, or use the copy below.

TAJ form View & download the declaration form Declaration by Certifying Official, single page. Print it and take it to the official who certifies your photo.

The addresses must match

The residential address on the declaration form must match the address on your TRN application line for line. Any difference and your application will be rejected.

How it works

The step-by-step process

  1. Certify your photo

    Have an approved official write TAJ’s wording on the back of one passport-size photo, with their name, title, signature, date and stamp.

  2. Complete the declaration form

    The same official fills in your full legal name and how many years they have known you, then signs and stamps it.

  3. Match your address

    Fill in your TRN application using exactly the same residential address as the declaration form.

  4. Submit at the tax office

    Take your birth certificate, certified photo, declaration and signed application to the tax office counter. You don’t have to go yourself: TAJ accepts applications submitted by a bearer, such as a family member or friend, on your behalf, as long as they bring your complete package.

Getting a TRN for a child

There is no minimum age for a TRN, and no fee. Children often need one for school, exams, health cards and savings accounts. The application must be completed and signed, or co-signed, by a parent or legal guardian.

When a parent signs

Parent’s name must be on the birth certificate

Bring the child’s birth certificate, a certified photo or picture ID for the child, and your own ID and TRN. The birth certificate must show the name of the parent who signs.

When a guardian signs

Bring proof of guardianship

Bring the same documents, plus a court order, or a Voluntary Declaration from the guardian together with one from an attestor who knows of the guardian and child relationship.

If the child has no passport or school ID, use a professionally produced passport-size photo certified by an approved official, such as a JP, doctor, attorney, minister of religion or school principal, dated within the last six months.

Applying for a TRN from overseas

You don’t have to travel to Jamaica. TAJ offers two routes:

  1. Apply online

    Create a login on the TAJ website and select “Apply for TRN”. Complete the web application, then print and sign the application it generates.

  2. Or use the paper form

    Download the TRN application from the TAJ website, complete it and sign it.

  3. Mail it with notarised ID

    Post the signed application to TAJ with a notarised copy of your passport or driver’s licence.

Avoid these

Major rejection traps

These mistakes send people home to start again.

The plain signature trap

A signature alone on the back of your photo is not enough. Without TAJ’s wording, the official’s name, title, signature, date and stamp, the photo will not be accepted.

The old certification trap

A photo certified more than six months ago will be refused. Get it certified close to the date you apply.

The missing parent name trap

For a child’s TRN, the birth certificate must show the name of the parent who signs the application. If that parent’s name is missing, a legal guardian must apply instead, with a court order or the two voluntary declarations.

Requirements checked against Tax Administration Jamaica’s TRN guidance in September 2026. TAJ can change them without notice, so confirm with us before you go.
