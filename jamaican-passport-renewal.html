Lane 1: 100% Concierge (Adult Renewals)

We can act as your proxy and handle the entire submission IF you meet all of these criteria. Please verify your eligibility below:

1. Are you an adult (18 years or older)?

2. Do you have your current, undamaged passport in your possession?

3. Was your passport issued after September 2001?

4. Is your legal name exactly the same? (No marriage/divorce changes)

5. Is your signature exactly the same?

6. Can you provide a digital (JPEG) passport-sized photograph?

⚠️ Based on your answers, PICA requires physical document processing or an in-person application. You do not qualify for the 100% Concierge Service.

Lane 2: Complex Cases & First-Time Applicants

If you answered "No" to any questions in Lane 1, PICA requires physical original documents or an in-person appearance. We prepare the complete, error-free file for you if you fall into these categories:

  • Minors: Children under 18 years of age.
  • Name Changes: Updating your name due to marriage, divorce, or Deed Poll.
  • Lost, Stolen, or Damaged: Passports that are missing, torn, washed, or damaged.
  • First-Time Applicants: Adults applying for their first Jamaican passport.
  • Other Adult Renewals: Passport issued before September 2001, a changed signature, or no digital photo available.
View Required Documents by Scenario

1. Minors (Under 18)

  • Completed physical application form signed by parent/legal guardian.
  • Two (2) physical passport-sized photographs (one certified by JP/Notary).
  • The child's Original Birth Certificate or Adoption Register.
  • Parent/guardian's valid Photo ID (Passport, Driver’s Licence, or National ID).
  • Note: The parent's name on their ID must exactly match the parent's name on the child's birth certificate. If it has changed (e.g., due to marriage), the parent's Marriage Certificate is also required.
  • The child's expired passport (if renewing).

2. Name Changes (Marriage, Divorce, or Deed Poll)

  • Completed physical application form.
  • Two (2) physical passport-sized photographs (one certified by JP/Notary).
  • Original Birth Certificate.
  • Original Marriage Certificate, Divorce Decree, or registered Deed Poll proving the name change.
  • The expired passport.

3. Lost or Stolen Passports

  • Completed physical application form.
  • Two (2) physical passport-sized photographs (one certified by JP/Notary).
  • Original Birth Certificate.
  • Valid Government-issued Photo ID (to establish identity).
  • An official Police Report confirming the passport was reported lost or stolen.

4. Damaged Passports

  • Completed physical application form.
  • Two (2) physical passport-sized photographs (one certified by JP/Notary).
  • Original Birth Certificate & Valid Government-issued Photo ID.
  • The Damaged Passport itself must be presented.
  • A written Affidavit explaining exactly how the passport was damaged, signed and stamped by a Justice of the Peace or Notary Public.

5. First-Time Adult Applicants

  • Completed physical application form.
  • Two (2) physical passport-sized photographs (one certified by JP/Notary).
  • Original Birth Certificate.
  • Valid Government-issued Photo ID.

Passport Questions

Which lane am I in?

Answer the six eligibility questions in Lane 1. If every answer is Yes, you qualify for the 100% Concierge renewal. If any answer is No, PICA needs original documents or an in-person application, and our Lane 2 package preparation service applies.

I live overseas. How will I get my new passport?

Lane 1 concierge renewals are dispatched by PICA via DHL to the address on your application. For Lane 2, you lodge the package at your nearest Jamaican Embassy, High Commission or Consulate, and that office tells you how the passport is returned: collection in person, or return by courier or prepaid envelope where they offer it. We confirm your office's return method before you submit.

I live in Jamaica. How do I collect my passport under Lane 2?

You collect it in person at the PICA office where you submitted the package, bringing your receipt and valid photo ID.

Do I need to mail you my physical photos?

If you qualify for Lane 1 (100% Concierge), no. We only need a digital JPEG photograph. For Lane 2, physical photos are required, but you keep them and take them with your package.

Do my photos need to be signed by a Justice of the Peace (JP)?

For Lane 1 (adult renewal, no name change), a JP signature is no longer required on the photo. For all Lane 2 cases, one photo must be certified by a JP or Notary Public.

Can you guarantee my passport will arrive by a certain date?

No. We submit or prepare your application swiftly, but PICA handles official processing and the courier or your submitting office handles delivery. Start your application well ahead of any travel date.

Are government fees refundable?

No. PICA fees are paid to the agency and are non-refundable. Our service fee is separate and is covered in our Terms & Conditions.

Where can I read more about PICA's rules?

For an in-depth breakdown of the whole process, read The Complete Jamaican Passport Application & Renewal Guide. For general questions about how we work, see our main FAQ.
