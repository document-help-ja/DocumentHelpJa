Why must I go in person for complex cases?

PICA maintains strict identification and security standards. Lost and damaged passports trigger a mandatory investigation by PICA officers, which requires speaking directly with the applicant. Applications for minors require the parent to be physically present to prevent custody disputes.

Furthermore, paper applications require the submission of your original, physical IDs and birth certificates. To protect your most sensitive documents, we prepare the perfect package for you, but you maintain possession of your IDs and hand them to the PICA agent yourself.

*Proxy Submission Exception: If you absolutely cannot attend in person, proxy submission may be arranged via a special consultation, provided you are willing to temporarily surrender your original photo ID to our office and sign a matching legal authorization.
