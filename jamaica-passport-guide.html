Prefer to watch?

A short walkthrough of passport renewals and replacements.

Photos are one of the most common reasons applications get sent back. Check yours against PICA's rules:

  • Size and finish: Two identical colour photos, 35mm x 45mm, matte finish, taken within the last six months.
  • Background: Plain, with no shadows, objects or other people. Dark complexion: white background. Light complexion: pastel background. Grey or white hair: pale blue or pastel.
  • Clothing: Light-complexioned applicants should avoid white. Avoid bright colours, and keep shoulders and chest covered.
  • Face: Full front view of head, neck and top of shoulders. No tinted lenses or heavy frames. Headgear only for religious reasons (complete Section H of the form).
  • Condition: No creases, ink marks or embossed seals.
  • Certification: Online renewals must stay uncertified. Paper, minor and lost filings need 1 photo endorsed by the JP who signs Section C.

Source: PICA photo requirements (opens PICA's website)

Common Questions

Can I renew online if my passport expires in more than a year?

No. PICA only accepts online renewals for passports that have expired or will expire within one year. If you need a new passport sooner, visit a PICA office or call PICA's customer care centre on 876-754-PICA.

Can I renew my child's passport online?

No. Online renewal is only for adults renewing a previously issued adult passport. Minors must apply on paper at a PICA office, or at a Jamaican embassy, consulate or high commission overseas.

Can I apply online if my passport was lost or stolen?

No. Lost and stolen passports need an in-person application at a PICA office or a Jamaican embassy, consulate or high commission, with a police report.

How do I pay for an online renewal?

By Visa or Mastercard (debit or credit). If your payment doesn't go through, print your application and take it to a PICA office or overseas mission with your current passport, two passport photos and the fee.

I started an online application but didn't finish. Can I pick up where I left off?

Yes, within five working days, using the Resume option on PICA's online portal. After that, contact PICA's customer care centre on 876-754-PICA.

Can my new passport be delivered to my home?

Yes, if you renew online. Addresses in Jamaica are usually delivered within seven working days; overseas deliveries go by secure courier, typically within 20 working days. A delivery fee applies, which PICA's Fee Calculator shows for your address.
