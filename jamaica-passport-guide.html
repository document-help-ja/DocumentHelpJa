The Complete Jamaican Passport Application & Renewal Guide

PICA turns applicants away every day for choosing the wrong processing stream or missing a single endorsement. Find your exact scenario below and get your file right the first time.

Laura Clarke
Lead Administrative Concierge

Key Takeaways

  • The online portal is strictly for adults renewing an undamaged 10-year passport issued after September 2001.
  • Online renewal photos must remain strictly uncertified. Paper, lost, and minor filings require 1 photo endorsed by a JP.
  • Children aged 3 to 17 must attend the PICA counter in person. Minors under 3 do not need to attend.
  • A lost or stolen passport requires a stamped police report less than 6 months old—a JP affidavit alone will be rejected.

Which Scenario Are You?

Select your situation below to highlight the exact steps that apply to you:

Scenario 1: Fast Online Stream

Adult Online Renewal (Simplified Portal)

PICA's online portal lets adult Jamaicans in the island or abroad renew without visiting an office. You must meet every one of these conditions:

  • Eligibility: Adult (18+) holding an expired/expiring 10-year passport issued after September 2001.
  • Physical Booklet in Hand: You possess the current passport booklet undamaged to scan the bio-data page.
  • Details Unchanged: Your name, date of birth, and place of birth match your existing passport exactly.
  • Uncertified Digital Photo: Photos uploaded to the PICA portal must remain uncertified and unwritten. Facial-recognition software rejects photos stamped or signed on the back.
The Certified Photo Trap

"Photos uploaded to the online portal must stay uncertified. PICA's software rejects any digital upload that has been stamped or signed on the back."

Scenario 2: Full Paper Route

First-Time Adult Application (Paper Booklet)

If you have never held an adult Jamaican passport, or your previous passport was issued prior to 2001, you must file a paper application (Form passport-1):

  • Proof of Citizenship: Original RGD birth certificate, adoption certificate, or citizenship certificate.
  • TRN: Original TRN card or official Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) letter.
  • Valid Photo ID: Driver's licence, voter ID, or national ID.
  • Section C JP Certification: Signed and stamped by a JP in Jamaica (or consular officer / notary overseas).
  • Photo Endorsement: Exactly one (1) photo endorsed by the same JP who completes Section C.
Scenario 3: Minor Age Thresholds

Passports for Minors (Under 3 vs. 3 to 17 Years)

Applications must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian, with distinct rules based on the child's age:

Age Threshold At the Counter Signatures & Verification
Under 3 Years Child does not need to attend the PICA counter. Parent submits. Parent signs Section C. JP endorses 1 photo. No child signature.
3 to 17 Years Child must be physically present with parent/guardian. Child signs Section A if 12+. Parent signs Section C. JP endorses 1 photo.
The Parental Consent Trap

"If a parent's name is not on the child's RGD birth certificate, or a court awarded sole custody, certified legal guardianship papers or a consent affidavit must accompany the file."

Scenario 4: Name Adjustments

Legal Name Changes & Marital Updates

If your legal name has changed since your last passport was issued, submit physical documentary proof matching the update:

  • Married Applicants: Original RGD marriage certificate. Hyphenated names require birth and marriage certificates to align.
  • Divorced Applicants: Original Decree Absolute alongside the RGD birth certificate if reverting to a maiden name.
  • Deed Poll: Official RGD-registered Deed Poll document confirming the legal name change.
Scenario 5: Security Clearance

Lost, Stolen or Damaged Passports

PICA routes missing or damaged passport files through its Investigation and Surveillance Unit to prevent identity fraud.

  • Lost or Stolen: Visit a police station and get an official, stamped police report explicitly naming your lost Jamaican passport. Must be less than 6 months old.
  • Damaged: Surrender the damaged physical booklet and submit a statutory declaration explaining how the damage occurred.
Template: Declaration for Damaged Passport

I, [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME], residing at [YOUR CURRENT ADDRESS], do solemnly and sincerely declare as follows:

1. I am a citizen of Jamaica and the holder of Jamaican passport number [PASSPORT NUMBER or "UNKNOWN"].

2. On or about [DATE OF INCIDENT], the said passport was damaged when [EXACT REASON, e.g., "accidentally washed in laundry"].

3. I am surrendering the damaged booklet herewith for cancellation and replacement.

Signature of applicant: _______________________
Declared before me on this ____ day of ____________, 20__.
Justice of the Peace / Notary Public: _______________________ [SEAL/STAMP]

PICA Photo Rules

Over 40% of PICA counter turn-aways come from non-compliant photographs. Check yours against these rules:

  • Dimensions: Exactly 35mm x 45mm matte print with a clean white background.
  • Facial Visibility: Both ears clearly visible; hair tucked back from eyebrows and cheeks.
  • Attire: No white clothing, no dark/tinted glasses, no heavy jewellery.
  • Certification Rules: Online renewals must remain uncertified. Paper/Minor/Lost filings require 1 photo endorsed by the JP who signs Section C.
